The Standard

Every unit is graded the way an engineer would grade it — not the way a seller would.

Most refurbished listings tell you a grade. PoeDrops tells you the reading: cycle count, hinge tension, keyboard travel, coating wear. It's the difference between "good condition" and knowing exactly what that means.
